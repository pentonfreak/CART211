THE GUILD
CONTRACT

Before taking your first quest, the Guild requires a signature. The clerk smiles: “It's standard procedure. Nobody has ever had a problem.”

Article I · The Fellowship

The Member acknowledges that the Guild exists to facilitate quests, promote camaraderie and recommend appropriate opportunities for all registered adventurers.

Article II · The Ledger

For the purpose of service improvement, the Guild may record mission selections, equipment preferences, time spent in deliberation, reward preferences, and any publicly or privately expressed inclination relevant to future quest allocation.

Article III · Forecasting

The Guild reserves the right to construct speculative profiles from these observations. Such profiles may influence future recommendations, rewards and the availability of certain missions.

Article IV · Guild Partners

Affiliated merchants may be furnished with hypothetical forecasts concerning what the Member could reasonably be expected to desire. The Member is not assured knowledge of the formulas behind such forecasts.

Article V · Consent

The Member is invited to consider the distinction between participation in a quest and the commercial uses of information produced by that participation.

Appendix XXVII · Small Print
