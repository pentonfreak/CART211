The Tiny Blue Dot

Cosmic perspective / one civilization

We are separated by borders, histories and beliefs. Seen from afar, we share one fragile home.

SOL SYSTEM / IIIONE PLANET · MANY STORIES

Not by many nations. By one civilization.

From the distance of space, our political lines vanish. Our shared home remains. This is where our journey begins.

01 / PERSPECTIVE

A single home, countless perspectives.

Voyager 1 photographed Earth from billions of kilometres away in 1990. At that distance our cities, borders and disputes disappear into a speck of light. The image is a reminder of shared vulnerability—not proof that cultures or countries should cease to exist.
