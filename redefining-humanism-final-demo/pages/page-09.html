09 / RESPONSIBILITY

The frontier is a question of character.

Interplanetary exploration would demand scientific ingenuity, but also restraint, cooperation and humility. This first-contact story is speculative. It asks what ethical principles humanity should carry into unfamiliar environments—and what it might need to learn first.

“Reaching farther is not the same as becoming wiser.”
