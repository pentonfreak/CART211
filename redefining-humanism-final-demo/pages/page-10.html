A QUESTION, NOT A FINAL ANSWER

What kind of humanity should we become before we reach the stars?

Technology may enlarge our capabilities. Whether it deepens our wisdom, dignity and cooperation remains our responsibility.

This site draws inspiration from Zuboff's critique of surveillance capitalism and expands into the author's broader philosophical questions. It does not claim that Zuboff proposed these future scenarios.

10 / CONTINUATION

The future remains unwritten.

This project begins with Zuboff's questions about surveillance and human autonomy, then widens to ask how a civilization might defend dignity, welcome difference and pursue the stars. Your answers are not a score. They are an invitation to keep thinking.
