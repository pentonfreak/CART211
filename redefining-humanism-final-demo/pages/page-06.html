A system might prevent violent conflict, but only by monitoring every citizen. What should come first?

06 / ETHICS

Different paths, shared questions.

Religious traditions ask what we owe to one another. Philosophers examine virtue, justice and good judgment. Science helps us test claims about the world, although evidence alone does not decide what should count as good. A humane future requires dialogue across these perspectives—not forced agreement.

Philosophical influences: Plato, Republic; Aristotle, Politics and Nicomachean Ethics; biblical traditions of justice and care.
