Who Holds the Power?

Corporate knowledge / surveillance capitalism

What do we know about a platform, and what might it infer about us?

THE INDIVIDUAL KNOWS
◯
  • The content they clicked
  • The answers they selected
  • The service they intended to use
THE PLATFORM MAY INFER
⌘
  • Patterns of attention and interest
  • Probable future behavior
  • Potential commercial value
  • Ways to shape future engagement

This summarizes a central concern in Shoshana Zuboff's account of surveillance capitalism. Inferences depend on the platform and the data it actually has.
